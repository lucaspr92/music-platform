# Producer mode: how I stop making "anything" and start making decisions

Lucas asked to replace him as producer. That means deciding the music, not deriving it from
contaminated measurements. Here is why the last attempts failed and what changes.

## Why v9-v12 were "anything"

1. **I derived instead of deciding.** The parts came from statistical modes of a transcript whose
   source was 88% kick bleed. A producer decides "this needs an offbeat bass and a tribal
   conversation", then checks it against the reference.
2. **I never worked at the level where music is decided: the 8-bar loop.** I handed over six
   minutes of everything at once. A track is a good loop plus arrangement.
3. **My metric approved noise.** "Onsets on the right sixteenth" is satisfied by garbage. It can
   sanity-check, it cannot judge.
4. **I brought no genre knowledge.** Now there is some, and it is sourced:
   Gino Da Koda, Le Modo - Tudm, **Lisa's Kickback, Tech House, 128 BPM, D minor, 6:01** (Oct 2025).
   The artist's profile: driving tech house, stripped-back minimal deep tech. Genre conventions:
   **offbeat hat**, **bass on one note with syncopation and a low-pass around 150 Hz**, tribal drop
   built on short and long percussion.
5. **No fast decision point.** A producer proposes options and gets a verdict in 30 seconds.

## The plan

**P0. Unit of work: 8 bars.** No six-minute render until the loop stands on its own. Verdict from
Lucas on each loop: yes or no, in half a minute.

**P1. Track sheet, decisions declared:** 128 BPM, D minor, 6:00. Kick 4/4 (the one measured
thing), clap 2 and 4, **offbeat hats**, **bass on one note with syncopation under a 150 Hz
low-pass**, tribal percussion in conversation, percussive breakdown as the track's moment.
Identity: stripped-back, driving. The three sounds that carry it: kick, bass, percussion.

**P2. Three 8-bar loops, same drums, different bass.** A: offbeat 8ths. B: rolling 16ths.
C: syncopated three-note figure. Lucas picks one. One variable changes at a time.

**P3. Arrangement with an energy arc:** percussive intro, kick enters, build (filter + riser),
main, tribal breakdown without kick, drop, outro. Fills every 8 bars, layers in and out.

**P4. Sound design of the three sounds that matter:** kick EQ and saturation, bass with a 150 Hz
low-pass plus glide, percussion EQ and panning.

**P5. Mix and master last**, with matchering as the reference target.

**P6. The loop that closes the cycle:** I decide and justify, Lucas vetoes. Every veto becomes an
explicit rule ("the bass never sits on the one"), so the taste transfers instead of the blind
iteration repeating.
